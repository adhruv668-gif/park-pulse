ABOUT PARK PULSE

Better parking is better city time.

Park Pulse is a hackathon project that makes it easier for drivers to see availability, reserve a space, and manage parking without friction.

MISSION

Reduce parking stress

Turn a frustrating search for parking into a simple digital journey.

DESIGN

Make information clear

Separate each step, show live status, and explain costs before a driver makes a decision.

TECHNOLOGY

Built to grow

Express APIs power the booking flow, while the chatbot is an independent Web Component microfrontend.
